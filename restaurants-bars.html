Questions

Questions bar owners ask

Does this replace my POS?

No. Keep your register. We plug into it and handle everything around it: bookings, marketing, staff and stock. Sales, voids and tips flow in on their own, so nobody types anything twice.

Which registers does it work with?

Union POS today, with Clover and Square next. If you use something else, tell us in the free audit and we'll tell you straight what's possible.

What does it cost?

Every build is priced after the free audit, with an exact price up front. One price covers the system, your website, hosting, maintenance and support. There are no add-on fees.

Will my staff actually use it?

It's built for a phone and written in plain words. Bartenders, servers, security and the kitchen each get their own simple screen, and we train everyone before day one.

Do I have to switch everything at once?

No. We start with the leak that's costing you the most, usually no-shows or untracked pours, then add what you'll actually use.

Can you handle our flyers and promo videos too?

Yes. Flyers, motion flyers, promo videos, menus and banners are an optional add-on from the team behind NU Flyers.
