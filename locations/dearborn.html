Doing business in Dearborn

What we see in Dearborn

Dearborn has some of the best-known food in Michigan. Warren Avenue and Michigan Avenue are lined with restaurants, bakeries, cafés, markets and family businesses, many run across generations, with Ford's world headquarters and The Henry Ford bringing visitors in every day.

Busy family kitchens tend to lose money in the details: catering orders taken by phone and written down, food costs that change weekly, and big holiday rushes that bring crowds but no way to reach those customers again.

Where the money goes

Three leaks we find in Dearborn

Phone and paper orders

Catering and large orders written down by hand, with no deposit and no record.

Food costs nobody updates

Prices from suppliers change, menu prices don't, and the margin quietly shrinks.

Holiday crowds that don't return

Big seasonal rushes with no list to invite those customers back.
