Doing business in Ann Arbor

What we see in Ann Arbor

Ann Arbor's business calendar follows the University of Michigan. Football Saturdays pack State Street and Main Street, graduation weeks book every table, and summer can go quiet. Kerrytown shops, cafés and service businesses ride the same waves.

The challenge is keeping revenue steady when the crowd turns over every year. That means capturing customers while they're here, filling slow weeks, and making every busy weekend count.
