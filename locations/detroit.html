Doing business in Detroit

What we see in Detroit

Detroit runs on independent businesses: restaurants and bars Downtown and in Corktown, salons and boutiques along Livernois, vendors at Eastern Market, and service businesses in every neighborhood. Most of them are run by owners who wear every hat.

We're Detroit-born. Good Cakes and Bakes on the Avenue of Fashion is one of the businesses we've grown with. What we see most here isn't a lack of customers. It's money leaking through no-show reservations, untracked comps, and customers who came once and never heard from the business again.

Where the money goes

Three leaks we find in Detroit

Weekend no-shows

Tables and booths held for parties who never come, with no deposit to cover the empty seats.

One-time customers

Game-day and event crowds come in once. Without their number, there's no way to bring them back.

Too many apps

Separate tools for bookings, texts, payroll and stock, none of them talking to each other.

Questions from Detroit owners

Do you work with businesses in every Detroit neighborhood?

Yes. We work with owners Downtown, in Midtown, Corktown, New Center, along Livernois, on the east side and the west side. Audits happen by phone or video, so where you're located doesn't change anything.

Can the software work with the register my Detroit restaurant already uses?

Yes, if you're on Union POS it connects today, and Clover and Square are next. You keep ringing up sales the way you do now. The system pulls sales, voids and tips in on its own, so nobody types anything twice.
