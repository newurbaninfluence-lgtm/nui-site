Doing business in Warren

What we see in Warren

Warren is Macomb County's largest city and one of Michigan's industrial centers. The GM Technical Center, suppliers and shops fill the business corridors, and restaurants, auto shops and contractors serve the neighborhoods.

For most businesses here, the work is steady. The money slips out in the admin: missed calls during the workday, jobs scheduled by memory, and invoices chased by hand.
