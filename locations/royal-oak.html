Doing business in Royal Oak

What we see in Royal Oak

Royal Oak is one of Metro Detroit's busiest nights out. Main Street, Washington and Fourth Street draw crowds for dinner, drinks and shows, and the Farmers Market keeps weekends moving. Competition for those customers is right next door.

With that much foot traffic, the leak usually isn't new customers. It's knowing who came in, getting them back on slower nights, and keeping a handle on costs when the bar is three deep.

Questions from Royal Oak owners

Questions owners ask

How do Royal Oak bars collect guests' numbers without slowing service?

Through bookings, guest lists and rewards sign-ups that happen on the guest's own phone. The numbers land in your system with permission to text them.

Can I send a text to fill a slow night?

Yes. Write it once, pick who gets it (regulars, birthdays this month, last week's guests) and send it. You'll see how many booked from it.

Will this replace my POS?

No. Keep your register. We connect to it (Union today, Clover and Square next) and handle everything around it.

It's priced after the free audit, with an exact price up front that covers the system, website, hosting, maintenance and support.
