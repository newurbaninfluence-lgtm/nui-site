How it works

From first call to running in three steps

No long contracts to read first, nothing to install yourself. We look at your business, show you the plan and price, then build it and train your team.

Book your free auditGet the demo

Step 1

Book your free audit

About 30 minutes by phone or video. We ask how you run today and look for where money slips out.

  • What you pay for each month, app by app
  • How customers find you, book and come back
  • Where staff time, stock or sales go missing
  • You leave with a plain list of leaks, yours to keep

What we usually find

People who look but don't buy, customers who never come back, and costs nobody is watching. Most owners have at least two of the three.

Step 2

Get your plan and price

Within a few days you get a clear plan: which leak we fix first, what we'll build, and exactly what it costs.

  • One price for the system, website, hosting, maintenance and support
  • Which apps you can cancel once it's live
  • No surprises and no add-on fees later

Start small if you want

You don't have to switch everything at once. We start with the leak that's costing you the most, then add what you'll actually use.

Step 3

We build it and train your team

Most builds take a few weeks. We set it up with your real menus, staff, customers and prices, so day one isn't an empty screen.

  • Your website and your back office, connected
  • Plugs into your register: Union today, Clover and Square next
  • Training for you, your GM and your staff
  • Support from a real person after launch

See it first

Get the demo

We're finishing a demo you can click through yourself: a sample bar with its bookings, staff, stock and morning report. Leave your number and we'll text you the link as soon as it's ready.

Questions

Questions about getting started

Is the audit really free?

Yes. About 30 minutes by phone or video. You keep the list of leaks we find whether you hire us or not.

What do I need to bring?

Nothing formal. It helps to know which apps you pay for each month and roughly what a good and a slow week look like.

How long until it's running?

Most websites and systems are built in a few weeks. We start with the leak that's costing you the most.

What happens after launch?

Hosting, maintenance and support are included. When something needs changing or fixing, a real person answers.

Let's find your leak.

One free audit. You'll leave knowing where your money is going, even if you never hire us.
